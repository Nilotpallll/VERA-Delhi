### Scene 3: The Live Scam Rescue Scenario (`1:15 – 2:30`)
#### *Scenario: "The ₹50,000 Telegram VIP Pool Trap"*
* **Story Narrative:**  
  *Ramesh, a retail investor in Pune, received a direct message on WhatsApp inviting him to an exclusive 'VIP High-Yield Institutional Trading Group'. The message promises:*  
  `"Join VIP Telegram group! Guaranteed 40% monthly returns on automated crypto mining pool. Send 50,000 INR to apexinvest@icici. Authorized by SEBI."`  
  *Unsure whether to send the ₹50,000, Ramesh pastes the message into VERA.*
* **On-Screen Actions:**
  1. Click the quick sample chip **"WhatsApp VIP Signals"** in the composer.
  2. The text area instantly populates with the suspicious message.
  3. Click **"Analyze & Screen for Fraud"**.
  4. The multi-phase forensic scanning progress bar triggers:
     - *Phase 1: Analyzing message text & syntax...*
     - *Phase 2: Extracting claimed entities & payment rails (`apexinvest@icici`)...*
     - *Phase 3: Cross-referencing authoritative SEBI registers...*
  5. The screen displays the **Verdict Card**:
     - **CRITICAL RISK (92.5 / 100)** banner in bold crimson.
     - **Red Flag Tag 1:** *SEBI Prohibition Violation: Guaranteed Returns Prohibited*.
     - **Red Flag Tag 2:** *Unregistered Advisory Entity / Fictitious Claim*.
     - **Red Flag Tag 3:** *Unregulated Third-Party Payment Gateway (`apexinvest@icici`)*.
* **Voiceover Narration:**
  > *"Watch VERA in action. A retail user pastes a suspicious stock tip promising a guaranteed 40% monthly return and requesting a ₹50,000 transfer to a private UPI address.*
  > 
  > *In under two seconds, VERA's deterministic pipeline fires. It detects three severe regulatory violations under SEBI's 2003 Fraudulent Trade Regulations, flags the fictitious entity, and issues a CRITICAL RISK verdict of 92.5 out of 100.*
  > 
  > *Ramesh halts the transfer. Fifty thousand rupees saved in a single click."*

## 🛠️ How to Record Locally via Screen Recorder

1. Ensure the web application is running:
   ```bash
   npm run dev:web
   ```
   *(Running at `http://localhost:3000`)*
2. Open your preferred browser (Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge) and navigate to:
   ```text
   http://localhost:3000
   ```
3. Open your screen recorder:
   - **Windows:** Press `Win + Alt + R` (built-in Windows Game Bar screen recording) or launch **OBS Studio** / **Loom**.
4. Follow the step-by-step clicks outlined in the storyboard above:
   - Start on the Cover screen.
   - Enter the main dashboard.
   - Click "WhatsApp VIP Signals" -> "Analyze & Screen for Fraud".
   - Show the 90+ Critical Risk score.
   - Toggle English / Hindi.
   - Open Veronica AI Assistant.
   - Click "Generate Official PDF Report".
   - Visit "Guidance" and "Emergency" tabs.
